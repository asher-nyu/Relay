Relay Device Mockup
4000 × 2662 pixels

Relay Device Mockup.svg is self-contained. Its two screenshots and Apple bezels are embedded, so it can be moved or shared as a single file.

Relay Device Mockup — Linked.svg uses the files in Screens and Bezels. To swap a screenshot, replace its PNG in Screens and reopen the linked SVG. Keep the MacBook screenshot at 3456:2234 and the iMac screenshot at 16:9; the layout preserves image proportions.

The background is its own named layer in both SVGs. Edit or replace that layer independently of the devices. Each device also has separate screen and hardware groups.

The device frames are Apple's unmodified original artwork from Apple Design Resources: MacBook Pro M5 16-inch Space Black and iMac M4 24-inch Green. The MacBook is placed in the foreground over the iMac’s lower-left area. Each device retains its original proportions; the laptop is enlarged to establish the foreground. Both screens’ main content remains visible.

The iMac screen is a new native screenshot of Relay's new-conversation page. Only Relay and wallpaper appear. The MacBook uses the supplied Design Review screenshot. Source screenshot files are preserved as PNGs.

Reference: https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/
